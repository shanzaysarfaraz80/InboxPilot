RETURNS - KNOWLEDGE BASE
=========================

Q: What is your return policy?
A: You can return most unopened or defective items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund or exchange. Items must be in their original packaging with all tags/accessories included.

Q: How do I start a return?
A: Reply to your order confirmation email or email returns@support.com with your order ID, the item(s) you want to return, and the reason. We'll send you a prepaid return label within 1 business day.

Q: Do I have to pay for return shipping?
A: Returns due to a defect, wrong item shipped, or damage in transit are free — we cover the shipping label. Returns for "changed my mind" reasons have a flat $5.99 return shipping fee deducted from your refund.

Q: Can I exchange an item instead of returning it?
A: Yes. Mention "exchange" instead of "refund" when you start your return and tell us the replacement item/size/color you'd like. Exchanges ship out as soon as we receive the returned item.

Q: What items cannot be returned?
A: Final-sale items, gift cards, personalized/custom products, and opened hygiene or perishable items cannot be returned unless defective.

Q: My order arrived damaged or wrong — what do I do?
A: We're sorry about that. Email returns@support.com with your order ID and a photo of the item. We'll send a free replacement or full refund and you do not need to send the item back in most cases.

Q: How long does a return take to process once you receive it?
A: Once we receive your returned item, inspection and refund/exchange processing takes 3-5 business days. You'll get an email confirmation when it's complete.
