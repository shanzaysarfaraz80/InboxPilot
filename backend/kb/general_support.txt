GENERAL SUPPORT - KNOWLEDGE BASE
=================================

Q: What are your support hours?
A: Our AI support is available 24/7. Human agents are available Monday–Friday, 9 AM–6 PM EST, and Saturday 10 AM–4 PM EST. For urgent security or billing matters, we offer 24/7 priority support for Premium subscribers.

Q: How do I contact support?
A: You can reach us via: (1) Email: support@support.com, (2) Live Chat: available at the bottom-right of every page (Mon–Fri, 9 AM–6 PM EST), (3) Phone: +1-800-SUPPORT (Premium plan only), (4) Help Center: help.support.com.

Q: Where can I find documentation / tutorials?
A: Visit docs.support.com for full documentation. We also have video tutorials on our YouTube channel (search "SupportCo Tutorials") and a community forum at community.support.com.

Q: What are the different subscription plans?
A: We offer: (1) Free — basic features, 5 GB storage, community support; (2) Pro ($19/mo) — all features, 100 GB storage, email support; (3) Business ($49/mo) — unlimited storage, priority support, team management; (4) Enterprise (custom pricing) — dedicated account manager, SLA, custom integrations.

Q: How do I give feedback or suggest a feature?
A: Submit feature requests and feedback at feedback.support.com. You can also upvote existing suggestions. Our product team reviews top-voted ideas each quarter.

Q: Do you have a mobile app?
A: Yes! Download on iOS (App Store) and Android (Google Play) — search "SupportCo". The mobile app includes all core features plus push notifications and offline mode.

Q: How do I invite team members?
A: Go to Settings → Team → Invite Members. Enter email addresses (comma-separated for bulk invite). Team members receive an invitation email and can join immediately. Admin rights can be assigned per member.

Q: What countries do you support?
A: Our service is available in 150+ countries. Billing is in USD, EUR, or GBP. Language support: English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Japanese, and Chinese (Simplified).

Q: How do I import data from another platform?
A: Go to Settings → Import Data and select your source platform. We support imports from CSV files, as well as direct integrations with Salesforce, HubSpot, Zendesk, and Intercom. Full import guides are at docs.support.com/imports.

Q: What is your SLA (Service Level Agreement)?
A: Response times by plan: Free (best effort, up to 72 hrs), Pro (24 hrs), Business (8 hrs), Enterprise (2 hrs with dedicated support). Uptime SLA: 99.9% for paid plans (compensated with service credits if not met).

Q: Do you offer non-profit or educational discounts?
A: Yes! We offer 50% discounts for registered non-profits and 30% for educational institutions. Apply at support.com/discounts with proof of status.
