SECURITY ISSUES - KNOWLEDGE BASE
==================================

Q: I think my account has been hacked / compromised.
A: Act immediately: (1) Change your password NOW at Account Settings → Security → Change Password, (2) Enable Two-Factor Authentication if not already on, (3) Review recent login activity at Account Settings → Security → Login History, (4) Revoke all active sessions (Security → Active Sessions → Revoke All), (5) Contact security@support.com immediately. Our Security team handles compromised accounts with top priority.

Q: I received a suspicious email claiming to be from your company.
A: This is likely a phishing attempt. Do NOT click any links or provide any information. Our emails always come from @support.com domains. Forward the suspicious email to security@support.com so we can investigate and take action.

Q: There is suspicious activity on my account.
A: (1) Immediately change your password, (2) Enable 2FA, (3) Go to Security → Login History to review all access, (4) Revoke sessions you don't recognise, (5) Contact security@support.com with specifics (dates, IPs if visible). A human security agent will review your case.

Q: My financial information may have been exposed.
A: We take this extremely seriously. Contact security@support.com immediately with your account email. Our security team will: (1) Investigate the claim, (2) Notify you of findings within 24 hours, (3) Assist with any necessary remediation. We recommend also notifying your bank.

Q: How do I enable two-factor authentication?
A: Go to Account Settings → Security → Two-Factor Authentication → Enable. Choose between: (1) Authenticator App (recommended — Google Authenticator, Authy, Microsoft Authenticator), (2) SMS to your registered phone number. Save your backup codes in a secure location.

Q: What data do you store about me?
A: Per our Privacy Policy (available at support.com/privacy): we store your account info (email, name), usage data, and transaction records. We do not store raw payment card numbers — cards are tokenised via our PCI-DSS compliant payment processor. You can request a full data export at Account Settings → Privacy → Download My Data.

Q: How do I report a security vulnerability?
A: Please email security@support.com with details. We have a responsible disclosure policy and offer a bug bounty program. Do not publicly disclose vulnerabilities before we have had a chance to address them (90-day window).

Q: My 2FA device was lost/stolen. How do I recover access?
A: Use your backup recovery codes (provided when you set up 2FA) to log in, then disable and re-setup 2FA. If you don't have backup codes, contact security@support.com from your registered email address. Identity verification will be required.

Q: How does your platform protect my data?
A: We use: (1) AES-256 encryption at rest, (2) TLS 1.3 for data in transit, (3) SOC 2 Type II certified infrastructure, (4) Regular third-party penetration testing, (5) Zero-knowledge design for sensitive fields.
