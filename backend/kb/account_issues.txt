ACCOUNT ISSUES - KNOWLEDGE BASE
================================

Q: How do I reset my password?
A: You can reset your password by clicking "Forgot Password" on the login page. You will receive a reset link via email within 2–5 minutes. Check your spam folder if it doesn't arrive. The link expires after 24 hours.

Q: My account is locked. What should I do?
A: Accounts are automatically locked after 5 consecutive failed login attempts for security reasons. To unlock: (1) Wait 30 minutes for automatic unlock, or (2) Use the "Forgot Password" link to reset credentials, or (3) Contact our support team for immediate manual unlock.

Q: How do I update my email address?
A: Go to Account Settings → Profile → Edit Email. A verification email will be sent to both your old and new address. Click the confirmation link within 48 hours to complete the change.

Q: How do I change my username?
A: Navigate to Account Settings → Profile → Username. Usernames can only be changed once every 30 days. The new username must be 3–30 characters and can include letters, numbers, and underscores.

Q: How do I delete my account?
A: To permanently delete your account, go to Account Settings → Privacy → Delete Account. Please note: this action is irreversible and all your data will be removed within 30 days per our Privacy Policy.

Q: I can not log in even with the correct password.
A: Please try the following: (1) Clear your browser cache and cookies, (2) Try a different browser or incognito mode, (3) Check that Caps Lock is off, (4) Ensure you are using the correct email associated with the account, (5) Reset your password using the "Forgot Password" option.

Q: How do I set up two-factor authentication (2FA)?
A: Go to Account Settings → Security → Two-Factor Authentication and follow the setup wizard. We support authenticator apps (Google Authenticator, Authy) and SMS-based 2FA.

Q: Can I have multiple accounts?
A: One account per person is permitted per our Terms of Service. If you need a business account in addition to a personal one, please contact our enterprise team.

Q: My verification email is not arriving.
A: Check your spam/junk folder first. Add noreply@support.com to your contacts. If still not received after 10 minutes, click "Resend Verification Email" from the login screen or contact support.
