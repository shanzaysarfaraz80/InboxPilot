REFUNDS - KNOWLEDGE BASE
=========================

Q: How do I request a refund?
A: Refunds are available within 30 days of delivery for returned items, or immediately for cancelled orders that haven't shipped yet. Email refunds@support.com with your order ID and reason, or use the "Request Refund" button in your Order History.

Q: How long does a refund take to arrive?
A: Once approved, refunds are processed within 3-5 business days. It can then take an additional 2-7 business days for your bank or card issuer to post the funds, depending on your payment method.

Q: Will I get a full refund?
A: Yes, items returned in their original condition within the policy window receive a full refund of the item price. Original shipping charges are non-refundable unless the return is due to our error (wrong/defective item).

Q: Do I get refunded to my original payment method?
A: Yes, refunds are always issued to the original payment method used for the purchase. We're unable to refund to a different card, account, or as store credit unless you specifically request store credit.

Q: Can I get a refund without returning the item?
A: In some cases — such as a lost package, a defective item we ask you to dispose of, or a pricing error on our end — we can issue a refund without requiring the item back. This is decided case-by-case.

Q: Why hasn't my refund shown up yet?
A: First check that 5-7 business days have passed since you received our refund confirmation email. If it still hasn't appeared, check with your bank, as some institutions take longer to post refunds. If it's been over 10 business days, contact refunds@support.com with your order ID.

Q: Can a refund be partial?
A: Yes — if you're keeping part of an order, received a partially damaged shipment, or a return fee applies, you may receive a partial refund reflecting only the returned/affected items.
